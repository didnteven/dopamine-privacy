Privacy Policy – Daily Dopamine Dose

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Daily Dopamine Dose ("the app") is built and run by Todd Hall. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Summary

The app stores everything on your device. It has no account, no server and no analytics, and it does not request internet access. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data.

What the app stores (on your device only)

  • Your activity lists and daily draw history.
  • The apps you choose to limit, the limits you set, and how much time those apps have been used and how many times you opened them today.
  • Your reminder time and preferences.

This data stays in the app's private storage and is excluded from Android backup. Uninstalling the app deletes it. Settings also lets you erase it.

Accessibility service ("App blocker")

If you choose to turn it on, the app uses Android's Accessibility Service for one purpose: to detect which app is currently open so it can show a block screen once that app's daily allowance, set by you, is used up.

  • It receives only window-change events (the package name of the app in front).
  • It does not read screen text, passwords, messages or any content, does not take screenshots and does not perform gestures or taps for you (apart from the "Go Home" button you press).
  • Nothing it observes leaves your device.

While it is on, Android shows a small ongoing notification ("App blocker is active") so the system keeps the blocker running. You can turn it off at any time in Android Settings > Accessibility.

Usage Access permission

Optionally, Usage Access lets the app reconcile your app usage after the phone interrupts it. The data is processed on-device and is not transmitted.

Notifications

The app can show a daily reminder notification. It is optional and local.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
